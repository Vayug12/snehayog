🔁 Retention — Does the Curve Flatten?

Signup cohorts vs the weeks they came back in. Weekly windows are used instead of exact D1/D7/D30 because on a small user base a single day swings wildly on a handful of people. What matters is not any one number but whether the curve flattens — a curve heading to zero means no product-market fit, however good week 1 looks.

📅 Habit — How Often They Come Back

Distinct days each user opened the app in the last 28 days. Shown as a distribution, not an average — an average lets a handful of power users hide a long tail of one-time visitors. DAU/MAU is the single best habit number: it answers what share of monthly users show up on a given day.
