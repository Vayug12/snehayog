Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about Vayug's video experience, creator publishing, advertising, monetization, AI features, and agent integrations.

What is Vayug?

Vayug is a creator-first video platform for discovering, watching, publishing, and monetizing short-form and long-form video.

Is Vayug the same as Snehayog?

Yes. Vayug is the product-facing name, while Snehayog is the repository, project, and internal package identity.

Does Vayug support short and long videos?

Yes. Yug is the short-form discovery experience and Vayu is the long-form viewing experience. Series and episodes are supported where configured.

How do creators make money on Vayug?

Creators can monetize in Vayug by opening the Account tab, sharing Vayug with 2 friends using the refer button or uploading 2 videos, then using Setup Billing when it appears. After adding a UPI ID, eligible creators can earn from eligible ad impressions and receive 80% of eligible advertising revenue, subject to eligibility, ad availability, policy, payout setup, and payment requirements.

Is the 80% creator revenue share guaranteed income?

No. It is a configured share of eligible advertising revenue, not a guaranteed income, CPM, RPM, or promise that every video will receive ads.

Can advertisers run campaigns on Vayug?

Yes. Campaign, creative, targeting, payment, impression, click, and analytics workflows are included. Inventory, pricing, approval, and targeting depend on the current deployment.

Does Vayug support AI dubbing and translation?

The platform includes transcription, translation, and speech-synthesis workflows for dubbing when the relevant feature flags, AI engines, quotas, and deployment services are available.

Does Vayug generate videos with AI?

The backend includes an asynchronous integration with a VayugAI agent for prompt-driven video generation. It may be disabled, hidden, or unavailable in some builds.

Does Vayug support E2EE subscriber-only videos?

Yes. Creators can publish subscriber-only videos using end-to-end encrypted delivery. Protected content is encrypted on the creator/client side, encrypted per-subscriber key material is stored by the backend, and an authenticated eligible subscriber decrypts the video locally.

Can creators connect with subscribers outside the app?

Yes. Authenticated creators can export available subscriber names, email addresses, profile information, and subscription dates as a CSV for consent-based use with their own email, newsletter, or external communication tools. Privacy law, anti-spam rules, subscriber consent, and platform terms apply.

Can creators send notifications to subscribers?

Yes. Creator Tools can send a direct push alert to all or selected eligible subscribers, optionally with a target URL. Alerts respect notification preferences, require an active app/device token, and are currently limited to two sends per creator per day.

Can an AI agent upload videos to Vayug?

Yes. Authenticated workflows support presigned uploads, direct uploads, upload registration, processing status, and server-sent status updates.

Can Vayug videos be shared or embedded on the web?

Yes. Available videos have shareable pages at /video/{video-id} and embeddable player pages at /embed/{video-id}.

What features are available to viewers?

Viewers can discover and search videos, watch short and long content, like, comment, share, follow creators, save videos, recover watch position, receive notifications, and report content.

What can creators do on Vayug?

Creators can upload and manage videos, edit metadata, organize series and episodes, add quizzes where enabled, build profiles, grow followers, review analytics, and access revenue and payout workflows.

Which sign-in methods does Vayug support?

The backend supports Google Sign-In and phone/OTP authentication. The exact options shown depend on the current app build and configuration.

Is Vayug open source?

The repository is MIT-licensed. Production deployments, user data, private credentials, media, and third-party provider accounts are not automatically public because the code is open source.

Is Vayug available in every country?

Website, app distribution, payments, advertising, language features, and creator payouts can vary by country, region, eligibility, and deployment configuration.
