Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application listed on Google Play as Vayug – Monetize Content Day 1, package name com.snehayog.app. The app is developed, published, owned, and operated by Sanju yadav, the developer name shown on the Google Play store listing.

Vayug – Monetize Content Day 1 is a digital content hosting and advertising platform that allows users to upload and share user-generated content (UGC). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information in compliance with global privacy standards and Google Play policies.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, profile information, and device identifiers.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Videos, images, captions, and related metadata uploaded to the platform.
  • Location Data: With your explicit permission, we collect your device's precise location (latitude and longitude) to provide localized content, advertising, and community features. You can enable or disable location services at any time in your device settings.
  • Advertising Data: Information related to advertising and campaign activity.
  • Transaction Information: Payment confirmations and billing records processed through authorized third-party payment gateways. We do not store card or banking details on our servers.
  • Reward Program Information: We may collect additional verification details from eligible participants in the Creator Incentive Program to prevent identity misuse, fraud, duplicate accounts, or manipulation of platform incentives.
  • Moderation & Safety Data: Reports, flags, and related information submitted to maintain community safety.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve Vayug – Monetize Content Day 1.
  • To host, distribute, and moderate user-generated content.
  • To provide localized content and advertising via Location Data.
  • To process advertising and calculate performance metrics.
  • To calculate platform-based engagement rewards under our Creator Incentive Program.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, or policy violations.

3. Creator Incentive Program

Vayug operates a discretionary Creator Incentive Program. Incentives are calculated based on platform performance metrics such as views, engagement levels.

Incentives are platform-managed benefits and are not guaranteed earnings, salaries, employment compensation, or financial investment returns.

We may collect additional verification information from eligible participants in the Creator Incentive Program to prevent fraud, identity manipulation, or reward abuse.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with:

  • Authorized payment gateways for transaction processing.
  • Cloud hosting providers for secure data storage.
  • Regulatory authorities when legally required.
  • Subscriber Information Sharing: When you subscribe to a creator on Vayug, your name and email address will be shared with that creator. This allows creators to communicate with their audience directly. Creators are contractually obligated to use this information responsibly and only for legitimate communication purposes related to their content. You may unsubscribe from any creator at any time to revoke this access.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encryption and access control to protect user data. Sensitive financial information is handled only by certified third-party payment processors.

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, secure tokens, and local device storage to maintain user login sessions, ensure platform security, analyze app performance, and deliver relevant advertisements. By using Vayug, you consent to our use of these tracking technologies.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Vayug is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If we discover that a user under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete that information and terminate the account.

8. User Rights & Data Deletion

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal information.

Account Deletion: You can delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the Vayug app via the Settings menu. Alternatively, you can request data deletion by contacting us at support@snehayog.site. Upon request, we will purge your personal data from our active databases within 30 days.
