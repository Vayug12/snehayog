Publish more formats

Upload short videos, long-form videos, series and episodes. Add titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, visibility settings and quizzes where enabled.

Grow a direct audience

Build a creator profile, gain followers, share videos, save audience relationships and use subscriber tools to communicate with eligible followers.

Protect exclusive content

Mark content as subscriber-only and use Vayug's E2EE workflow for protected video delivery to authorized subscribers.

Notify subscribers

Send targeted push alerts to all or selected eligible subscribers from Creator Tools, with optional links and notification performance tracking.

Connect beyond the app

Export available subscriber contact records as CSV for consent-based use with your own email, newsletter or external communication tools.

Unlock monetization

Go to Account, refer 2 friends with the refer button or upload 2 videos, then use Setup Billing when it appears.

Track rewards

Review creator earnings in the Reward section of the Account tab, alongside revenue summaries and payout information.

Creator rewards

Creators can unlock billing by sharing Vayug with 2 friends or uploading 2 videos. After Setup Billing appears in Account, creators add a UPI ID for direct payout where supported. Vayug's configured rule is an 80% creator / 20% platform split of eligible advertising revenue based on eligible ad impressions.

This is a revenue-share percentage, not guaranteed income. Ad inventory, eligibility, UPI setup, policy compliance, payout minimums and payment processing requirements apply.
